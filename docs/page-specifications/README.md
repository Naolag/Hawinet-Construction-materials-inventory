# Page Specifications

The frontend currently includes visual routes for:

1. Dashboard — daily sales, gross profit, order count, category split, unpaid sales, weekly stock movement and inventory overview.
2. Products — parent-product catalog, brand under product name, category, variant access and product forms.
3. Inventory Management — base-unit stock, minimum stock, purchase/selling price per unit, inventory value and quick links to measurement units and item-specific conversions.
4. Transactions — one New Transaction area with six types: Stock Receipt, Sale, Customer Return, Supplier Return, Stock Increase and Stock Decrease; history includes paid, partially paid and unpaid sales examples.
5. Reports — inventory overview, low-stock and out-of-stock listings.
6. Measurement Units — shared reusable unit directory.
7. Unit Conversions — item-specific alternative-unit conversion factors and alternative-unit prices.
8. Categories — category directory and status.
9. Employee Management — employee directory and roles.
10. Settings — business details, regional preferences and transaction safeguards.

All sample records are illustrative. Forms are visual-only and do not persist data.
