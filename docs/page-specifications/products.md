# Products

Direct port of Framer ProductsWorkspace. Parent product catalog, search/category filtering, pagination, product details, add/edit product, activate/deactivate, delete confirmation, variants list/details, add/edit variant, category attribute creation, and loading/empty/no-results/no-variants states. Product name and category are required; duplicate names are checked case-insensitively; category is locked once variants exist. Variant combinations are unique and required category attributes must be completed.
